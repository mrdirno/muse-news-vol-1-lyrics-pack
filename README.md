# MUSE NEWS VOL. 1 — Lyrics Pack

Original lyrics by **Aldrin Payopay** (DRINOMAN), written September 24, 2026.

This repo is the timestamped original-creation record for the MUSE NEWS VOL. 1
lyrics — and a pack to send you off on a journey.

## The deal

The lyrics are yours now. The style is yours to invent.

These songs were made with Aldrin's personal Suno style preset turned on.
That preset can't be copied and the output can't be reproduced — not even by
him. So there are deliberately **no style prompts** in this pack. Bring your
own sound: your voice, your beat, your genre. Make it unrecognizable. That's
the point.

## What's here

- `lyrics/` — all 8 original lyric sheets, verbatim, exactly as written.
- `EP_TRACKS.md` — the 5 songs that made the EP.
- `UNFINISHED.md` — the 3 songs the crowd gets to tackle themselves.
- `MANIFEST` — sha256 fingerprints of every lyric file, so the original
  creation record is verifiable.

## How to participate

1. Pick a song from `UNFINISHED.md` (or reimagine an EP track your own way).
2. Produce your own version — any style, any sound.
3. Credit the original: "Lyrics by Aldrin Payopay (DRINOMAN), CC BY 4.0" and
   link back to this repo.
4. Share it. If it's great, the world will hear it.
